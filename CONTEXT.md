# Portfolio

This context defines the language used to present Marc Mathieu's work to frontend hiring teams.

## Language

**Primary Audience**:
Hiring teams filling senior frontend roles at design-conscious consumer product companies.
_Avoid_: General audience, anyone hiring developers

**Aspirational Employer**:
A scaled product company with mature engineering practices, such as Reddit, Netflix, or Asana.
_Avoid_: Series C or higher

**Anchor Case Study**:
A substantial account of a project that explains the problem, Marc's responsibility, the work shipped, and the available public evidence. AG1, BeautyNexos, and Battlefield Portal Builder for Battlefield 2042 are the initial Anchor Case Studies.
_Avoid_: Project card, client logo

**Selected Work**:
A shorter account of relevant work that has enough public evidence to support Marc's account but not enough material for an Anchor Case Study.
_Avoid_: Other stuff, miscellaneous projects

**Public Evidence**:
Public pages, public screenshots, and carefully attributed descriptions that do not expose proprietary code, internal diagrams, confidential metrics, or NDA-covered material.
_Avoid_: Proof, client assets

**Three Worlds**:
The portfolio’s visual identity: a dark typographic opening and three project chapters with distinct green, orange, and lilac art direction.
_Avoid_: Unrelated branded microsites, full-screen 3D spectacle

**World Overview**:
The opening arrangement of three responsive project panels that introduce AG1, Battlefield Portal Builder, and BeautyNexos.
_Avoid_: Logo wall, generic card gallery

**Panel Focus**:
A project panel expands on hover or keyboard focus while its neighbors remain available. Essential text and actions remain readable.
_Avoid_: Blur effect, hidden navigation

**Portfolio Narrative**:
The single continuous page through which visitors encounter Marc's positioning, work, career, and contact information.
_Avoid_: Homepage, project grid

**Case Study Chapter**:
One of three equally substantial sections covering AG1, Battlefield Portal Builder for Battlefield 2042, or BeautyNexos. Each chapter follows the same evidence structure while using project-specific art direction.
_Avoid_: Project card, work tile

**World Transition**:
The continuous visual entrance from a project panel into its chapter, with a return action that restores the visitor’s overview position.
_Avoid_: Intro gate, decorative machine

**Reading Zone**:
A stable part of a Case Study Chapter where scroll-driven motion pauses so visitors can inspect text, interfaces, and evidence.
_Avoid_: Static section

**Career Timeline**:
A concise account of Marc's career that connects capabilities to the work where they mattered without presenting a standalone skills grid.
_Avoid_: Résumé copy, skills cloud

**Contact Path**:
The final set of direct actions for `avianmathieu@gmail.com`, GitHub at `ludapops`, LinkedIn, and résumé download, without an on-site form or scheduling tool.
_Avoid_: Lead form, booking funnel

**Portfolio Navigation**:
The opening header and persistent World Switcher that provide direct access to the work, About, résumé, and contact.
_Avoid_: Full-screen menu, hidden links

**Project Journey**:
A concise explanation of public product behavior that helps readers understand Marc’s contribution.
_Avoid_: Invented internal architecture, unrelated interactive demo

**Evidence Caption**:
A note attached to current public imagery that records its capture date and states when the live product has continued evolving since Marc's work.
_Avoid_: Disclaimer, screenshot note

**Public Repository**:
The openly accessible `ludapops/marcmathieu.dev` repository whose history and documentation form part of the portfolio evidence.
_Avoid_: Source dump

**Portfolio Identity**:
Marc Mathieu's professional name as presented in the portfolio, résumé, metadata, and future domain.
_Avoid_: Avian Mathieu, studio name

**Positioning Statement**:
"Senior frontend engineer. Complex products, carefully made."
_Avoid_: Developer who thinks like a designer, creative developer

**Opportunity Fit**:
Senior frontend roles that are remote within the United States, hybrid in Miami, or hybrid in New York with relocation or regular travel.
_Avoid_: Freelance availability, general software roles

**Portfolio Domain**:
`marcmathieu.dev`, the intended public address for the portfolio.
_Avoid_: Portfolio URL, personal-site domain

**Release Scope**:
The first public release containing the three Anchor Case Studies with a content model that can accept future Selected Work without showing empty placeholders.
_Avoid_: Coming-soon grid, client-logo wall

**Hero Action**:
The project panels and Explore the work link that take visitors directly into the portfolio evidence.
_Avoid_: Intro interaction required before entry

**Résumé Download**:
The attached résumé published as `Marc-Mathieu-Resume.pdf` and linked from Portfolio Navigation after its project dates receive a final launch check.
_Avoid_: CV page, résumé section

**Project Accent**:
The consistent color assigned to each project: green for AG1, orange for Battlefield, and lilac for BeautyNexos.
_Avoid_: Random section colors

**Display Type**:
Oversized, tightly spaced sans-serif typography that gives Marc’s name and chapter headings their visual weight.
_Avoid_: Decorative type that compromises reading

**Motion Voice**:
Precise quick movement for navigation and focus, paired with slower cinematic movement for chapter transitions and no continuous movement inside Reading Zones.
_Avoid_: Bouncy motion, constant scroll effects

**Mobile Focus**:
Direct tap targets and stacked project previews designed for touch, without requiring hover.
_Avoid_: Device-tilt interaction, mobile cursor

**AG1 Chapter**:
The Anchor Case Study about Marc's 2023-2025 leadership in decoupling AG1's subscription experience, shared ownership of interface updates, and personal implementation of the Shop AG1 and cart flows.
_Avoid_: Shopify project

**Battlefield Chapter**:
The Anchor Case Study about Marc's mid-2020 to December 2021 work on Battlefield 2042's web-based Battlefield Portal Builder, including weapon selection, equipment selection, and custom Experience configuration. It does not claim work on the Rules Editor or game development.
_Avoid_: EA Sports project, game development, companion portal

**BeautyNexos Chapter**:
The Anchor Case Study about Marc's November 2025 to present ownership of cross-platform product initiatives, including Trade Calendar, payments and subscription states, products, galleries, and the member experience.
_Avoid_: Flutter project

**World Switcher**:
The persistent navigation connecting the overview, the three project chapters, and the Next World contact section.
_Avoid_: Theme picker, tabbed content

**Next World**:
The contact finale inviting a prospective team to build its next product with Marc.
_Avoid_: Contact form, booking funnel

## Public navigation language

Use “projects” in visitor-facing copy: “View project,” “All projects,” and “The next project.” Three Worlds remains an internal design name only.
